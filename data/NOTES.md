# Data decisions

- Test set: Global Voices news (yoruba-text/Iroyin), as in Orife et al. (2020). MENYO's own Global Voices files are excluded from training to prevent overlap.
- Excluded YorubaForAcademicPurpose: no tone marks.
- Excluded JW300: withdrawn over copyright.
- Excluded TheYorubaBlog: in a native-speaker spot check, 6 of 8 sampled sentences were wrongly or partly marked.
- Spot-check error rates for the sources kept: MENYO 1/8, LagosNWU 1/8, proverbs 2/8.
